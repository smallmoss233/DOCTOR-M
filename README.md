**English Introduction**

DOCTOR M combines the [**AIT**](https://modrinth.com/mod/ait) mod, [**AIT Extras**](https://modrinth.com/mod/ait-extras), and [**Create**](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-fabric), while also expanding AIT's adventure content.

It is recommended to install a [**Blue Archive-style Halo**](https://modrinth.com/mod/blue-archive-halo) mod, which will make the interiors of the Evereye and Watcher TARDIS models look even cooler.

*Watcher Console Texture Authors*: Smallmoss (me), 白桦下的赤俄

*Rainbow and Fire Interior Authors*: Asnit_PnQing

*Sig Interior Author*: 斯格.尔特

*Way Home Interior Author*: Time Lord与TARDIS type 90的千年虐恋

*Redstone Torch Sonic Screwdriver Author*: 慢跑者

*Evereye Interior & Sonic Screwdriver & Exterior, Watcher Interior, Insane Interior & Exterior Author*: Smallmoss (me)

You may include the DOCTOR M mod in your modpacks, but please be sure to credit the source. The interiors may not be ported to other mods and redistributed without permission.
