## **English**

DOCTOR M is a mod that combines the [**AIT**](https://modrinth.com/mod/ait) mod, [**AIT Extras**](https://modrinth.com/mod/ait-extras), and [**Create**](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-fabric).
It adds new structures and modifications designed **to make the AIT progression less tedious** (I don't want to spend hundreds of hours searching for blueprints anymore!!!)

Please note that this mod was originally created for a Doctor Who (DW) fan modpack with its own complete universe and lore. Therefore, there's no need to speculate about the identities of untranslated names like "Jinming"—just enjoy the experience as you see fit~

Watcher Console texture author: Smallmoss (myself), Red Russia under the Birch.

Rainbow and Fire Interior authors: Asnit_PnQing.

Sig Interior author: Sig. Erd.

Evereye Interior & Sonic Screwdriver & Exterior, Watcher Interior, Insane Interior & Exterior author: Smallmoss (myself).

You are welcome to include the DOCTOR M mod in your modpack, but you **must** credit the source. The interior designs **may not** be ported to other mods or redistributed without permission.
